# Audit: non-Rust assets and their licences (read 2026-11-04)

Scope: every non-Rust asset in the repository, in the release binaries and in the training pipeline of the native OCR model.
Method: file listing of the worktree and of the whole git history (all branches), reading the training scripts and ROADMAP rows,
and reading the upstream licence pages (links in the tables). Nothing was compiled. Nothing was deleted; only wrong
attributions were corrected (list at the end).

## RISKS (read this first)

**I am not a lawyer.** This is a technical reading of licence texts, written so that a non-lawyer can decide what to ask a
lawyer. Where I write "probably" it is a guess, not legal advice.

## 2. Provenance of `multi.lnm` in one paragraph (verified from the repo, not from the author)

Architecture and training code are the project's own (`train.py`). Stage 1 (model v1/v2, commits 80e14f0, b71793f): synthetic
lines only; text = words drawn from the wordfreq top-40k lists of the eight languages plus digits, symbols and dates;
glyphs = the Debian fonts of `FONT_GLOBS`; damage = noise, blur, tilt, JPEG, cedilla variants. Stage 2 (24e9fa3, 2026-11-03):
warm start from stage 1, 35 % real lines of magazine issue 06 (ABBYY labels). Stage 3 (e2efa43, 2026-11-03): 5,956 real lines
(issue 06, textbook pages, issues 04/05/07), 10,000 steps, BN frozen. The fonts actually installed during training are the
ones on this container (Debian packages named above); the repo does not record the exact list used per stage. The
numbers in ROADMAP 4.12 / 4.17 are the author's measurements and were not re-run here.

## 4. Could not verify

- Rights to *Revista StrING* issues 04-07 and to the 1960 textbook (nothing in the repo; needs the owner).
- Romanian copyright term for the textbook (law text not read).
- Upstream pages of GNU FreeFont (gnu.org, blocked), TeX Gyre / Latin Modern (gust.org.pl, blocked), GFS Baskerville, Hack, Ubuntu Font, Noto Emoji, Caladea (upstream URL 404): for these I used the licence files shipped in the installed Debian packages or in the egui crate, which are the texts that travel with the files, not the live upstream pages.
- The text of CC BY-SA 4.0 and the terms of OpenSubtitles / OSCAR / Google Books were not re-read; I relied on wordfreq's NOTICE.
- What docTR's pretrained weights were trained on beyond one maintainer comment (the docTR documentation page was not reachable).

- The legal effect of training on copyrighted or CC BY-SA material (open question; not decidable by reading licences).
- Whether the numbers in ROADMAP 4.12 / 4.17 are reproducible (not re-run; not my topic).
